# UK tidal rivers

Interactive map of eight British tidal rivers (Thames, Severn, Mersey, Humber/Ouse/Trent, Tyne, Clyde, Forth, Tay), traced from source to sea and coloured by fresh, brackish and salt water, with their main tributaries, the canals that connect them, tidal limits and approximate spring tidal ranges.

Includes an illustrated story for the whole set and for each river, on why these estuaries matter for nature and people, with sources.

**Live map:** https://tijlvandenberg.github.io/uk-tidal-rivers/

River and canal courses: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), simplified to ~100–150 m. Fresh/brackish/salt boundaries are typical averages and shift with river flow. Tidal ranges are rounded spring-tide values.
